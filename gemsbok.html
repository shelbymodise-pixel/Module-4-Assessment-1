Gemsbok

The gemsbok is a large antelope found in the arid regions of Southern Africa. It belongs to the genus Oryx. Gemsbok are well adapted to desert conditions and can survive with very little water. They can weigh up to 240 kg.

Gemsbok are easily recognized by their long, straight horns and striking black and white facial markings. Both males and females have horns, which they use for defense against predators.

Gemsbok are herbivores and feed on grasses, roots, and bulbs. They live in dry areas such as deserts and savannahs.
