Gorrilla

Gorillas are large primates and belong to the family Hominidae. They are found in the forests of central Sub-Saharan Africa. There are two main species: the Eastern gorilla and the Western gorilla. Male gorillas, known as silverbacks, can weigh up to 200 kg.

Gorillas have strong bodies, broad chests, and large hands. They are known for their intelligence and social behavior, living in groups led by a dominant male. They use their arms for movement and communication.

Gorillas are herbivores and mainly eat leaves, stems, fruits, and shoots. They live in forests and prefer dense vegetation.
